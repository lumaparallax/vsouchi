# Contributing

This is a single-maintainer project prioritizing architectural stability and deliberate pacing over rapid feature expansion. Issue triage and PR reviews are handled on a best-effort basis.

## Where to Start

| Topic | Channel | Guidelines |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bugs & unexpected behavior** | **Issues** | Open a new issue with environment details and steps to reproduce. |
| **Issue fixes & documentation updates** | **Pull Requests** | PRs are accepted for existing issue fixes and documentation updates. |
| **Questions, setups, & general help** | **Discussions** | Share setups, usage tips, and community troubleshooting. |
| **New features & additional subsystems** | **Not Accepted** | Feature PRs are not accepted; development follows the [Features & Scope](README.md#features--scope) roadmap. |
| **Android Background & OOM Kills** | **Out of Scope** | See [Phantom Process Docs](https://github.com/agnostic-apollo/Android-Docs/blob/master/en/docs/apps/processes/phantom-cached-and-empty-processes.md#commands-to-disable-phantom-process-killing-and-tldr) (Termux `Signal 9`) or [Don't Kill My App](https://dontkillmyapp.com/) to disable vendor specific battery restrictions. |

> [!NOTE]
> The core architecture is being redesigned for multi-node support. During this phase, contributions for immediate bug fixes and essential requirements are appreciated over new features.
